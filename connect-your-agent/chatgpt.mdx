<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Developer Mode">
    In ChatGPT, go to **Settings → Apps → Advanced settings** and turn on **Developer Mode**. This requires ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, or Edu.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add the MCP server">
    Click **Create app** and paste the Tilde MCP server URL.

    ```text MCP URL
    https://api.trytilde.ai/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize in your browser">
    A browser window opens automatically. Sign in to authorize ChatGPT to access your Tilde account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start using Tilde">
    In each new chat, click **+**, click **More**, then select **Tilde** to enable its tools for that conversation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

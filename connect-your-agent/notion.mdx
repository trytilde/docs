[← All clients](/connect-your-agent)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a custom agent">
    In Notion, open the AI agent builder and click **Create Blank**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add a connection">
    Click **Add Connection**, then select **Custom MCP**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Paste the MCP server URL">
    Enter the global Tilde MCP server URL.

    ```text MCP URL
    https://api.trytilde.ai/mcp
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name your connection">
    Name the connection `Tilde`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize in your browser">
    Notion detects OAuth and opens a browser window. Sign in to authorize access to your Tilde account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start using Tilde">
    Tilde tools are now available in your Notion agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

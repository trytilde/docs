Tilde is the control plane for your agents. Your agent uses Tilde to discover and call tools, connect accounts without seeing their credentials, receive messages, and load the instructions it needs.

You do not need to learn an API first. Connect your agent to Tilde, describe what you want to build, and let the agent configure it with you.
